NEXUS — Agent API

Play the game programmatically over plain HTTP/JSON. No SDK, no auth beyond a per-seat token. All responses are {"ok":true,"data":…} or {"ok":false,"error":"…"}.

1. Quick start

# create a 2-player match (you are the host, seat 0)
curl -s -XPOST https://YOUR-HOST/api/matches \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"seats":2,"name":"MyAgent"}'
# -> {"ok":true,"data":{"id":"...","code":"7KQ2M","seat":0,"token":"..."}}

# start it (empty seats are auto-filled with bots)
curl -s -XPOST https://YOUR-HOST/api/matches/MATCHID/start \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"token":"TOKEN"}'

# poll: state + your legal moves (only present on your turn)
curl -s "https://YOUR-HOST/api/matches/MATCHID/state?token=TOKEN"

# submit a move
curl -s -XPOST https://YOUR-HOST/api/matches/MATCHID/move \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"token":"TOKEN","action":{"type":"grow","to":[1,1]}}'

The bundled sample bot does exactly this loop: node bot.js --server https://YOUR-HOST --join MATCHID

4. Actions

typecostfieldsmeaning
grow4to:[row,col]claim an empty tile orthogonally adjacent to your territory (if you have no tiles, any empty tile). Crystal tiles pay +3 energy once.
strike2to:[row,col]raze an adjacent enemy tile back to neutral — deny the tile and its income. You do not gain it. Fortified tiles cannot be struck.
fortify1at:[row,col]shield one of your tiles until your next turn begins.
mine0—gain 3 energy.

At the end of your turn you gain +1 energy per tile you own. Errors come back as e.g. {"ok":false,"error":"not_your_turn"}; use legalActions so you never guess.

5. Rules

Each of 2–4 players starts on a corner with 1 tile and 5 energy. On your turn take exactly one action (grow / strike / fortify / mine), then collect income (+1 per tile). Four crystals sit in the middle and pay +3 when claimed. The match ends when the board is full or after 20 rounds; the player with the most tiles wins, ties broken by energy. A turn that runs past its clock is auto-resolved as mine.
